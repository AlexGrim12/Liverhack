# Liverpool Design System — El Puerto de Liverpool

This skill defines the visual identity, mobile-first design patterns, color tokens, and corporate UI guidelines for **El Puerto de Liverpool** digital platforms, specifically **Talento 360**.

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Colors

El Puerto de Liverpool's corporate and digital identity combines elegance, institutional authority, and dynamic retail energy:

1. **Morado Liverpool (`#7B2D6E`, `#5A2050`)**:
   - Primary institutional color. Used in emblems, primary action buttons, active navigation states, and progress bars.
2. **Naranja Liverpool (`#F47920`, `#D9610B`)**:
   - Dynamic accent color. Used for high-impact callouts, key selection pills, and important accents.
3. **Neutrals & Surfaces**:
   - Canvas: `#F7F5F6`
   - Card Background: `#FFFFFF`
   - Text Primary: `#241B22`
   - Text Muted: `#6B5F68`
   - Border Line: `#ECE7EA`

---

## 2. Design Principles: Intuitive & Mobile-First

1. **Uncluttered & Natural**:
   - Eliminate redundant badges, unnecessary sub-bars, and visual noise.
   - Every element must serve an immediate purpose.
2. **Mobile Navigation**:
   - Clean top navigation bar with Liverpool emblem and simple role switcher dropdown.
   - Clean, readable cards with comfortable padding.
3. **Direct Actions**:
   - Clear primary actions (e.g. "Publicar requisición", "Aprobar como finalista", "Agendar entrevista").
   - Multi-candidate comparison presented in clean criteria cards instead of wide horizontal-scrolling tables.
